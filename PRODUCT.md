# Product

## Register

product

## Users

GiProy Network is used by construction, engineering and administrative teams that manage projects, budgets, schedules, resources, marketplace flows and multi-company operations inside a live ERP. Users work in dense operational screens where speed, trust and continuity matter more than decoration.

## Product Purpose

GiProy centralizes the operational lifecycle of construction projects: companies, users, projects, EDT/EDO structures, APUs, budgets, schedules, reports, community and marketplace workflows. Success means preserving tenant-safe data, keeping classic workflows stable, and evolving the product incrementally without breaking existing operations.

## Brand Personality

Technical, reliable and orderly. The interface should feel professional, precise and work-focused, with enough visual warmth from the GiProy identity to avoid a generic enterprise shell.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style compositions, oversized hero sections, decorative cards, gratuitous gradients, visual divergence from the existing GiProy shell, and any BIM-first experience leaking into classic workflows while BIM is flagged off.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the classic ERP workflow first.
- Favor dense but readable operational surfaces.
- Keep visual hierarchy consistent across modules.
- Make state and tenant context explicit without adding noise.
- Improve incrementally with narrow, reversible slices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear contrast, stable layouts, keyboard-friendly controls and reduced-motion-safe behavior. Dense engineering screens should remain scannable on laptop displays and avoid relying on color alone for critical state.
